# 03 — Value Prop & Messaging — YC AI Founders (DEMO)

## Core value proposition
- **One-sentence promise:** Your entire market — every fit company + the decision-makers, enriched, plus the people already posting about your problem — in one clean list.
- **The "before" state:** you know you need pipeline, but the list doesn't exist and you don't have time to build it.
- **The "after" state:** your whole market in one place, ready for sales / partnerships / fundraising / hiring.

## Proof / credibility
- **[Your proof — who you've built this for.]** Lead with **selectivity**, not results: "I only reach out to products with a real audience worth going after."

## Messaging angles
- **Angle 1 — founder-to-founder:** a peer, not a vendor. "I'm a founder too."
- **Angle 2 — selectivity (the hook):** "I only reach out when a product clearly has an audience worth going after. Yours does."
- **Angle 3 — the intent layer (differentiator):** "…plus the people already posting about [the problem] on Reddit/LinkedIn."

## Tone & rules
- **Tone:** founder-to-founder, direct, warm. **Very short, punchy, specific.**
- **CTA:** "Want 20–30 that fit, to see it? No strings."
- **Banned:** long emails, "hope this finds you well," fake flattery, results/percentage claims.

## Competitors / alternatives
- Clay, Apollo, ZoomInfo, an in-house SDR, "I'll just build the list myself."
