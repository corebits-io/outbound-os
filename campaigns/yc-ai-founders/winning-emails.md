# Winning emails — YC AI Founders (DEMO)

> The short founder-to-founder opener `write-copy` works from. Must read **human**, not AI-written: no "fellow founder," no template rhythm, contractions, one real reason, very low friction.

## Email 1 — founder-to-founder
- **Subject:** a list for {{company}}
- **Body:** Hey {{first_name}} — I'm a founder too, so I'll keep this short. I only reached out because {{company}} clearly has an audience worth going after — people are already posting about {{problem}}. Here's what I do: I hand founders one list — the companies that fit your ICP, the decision-makers at each (enriched), plus the people already talking about {{problem}} on Reddit and LinkedIn. YC drills it into you — go talk to your users. This just hands you who they are. Want me to pull 20–30 that fit {{company}} so you can see it? Reply here and I'll send them over — no strings.
- **Why it works:** real, specific reason (their audience exists) + selectivity + a relatable YC nod + a one-line, zero-friction ask. Doesn't read like a template.

## The low-friction ladder (follow-ups)
- Day 3: "just reply 'yes' and I'll send the 20–30 over. If it's useful, I'll drop you a LinkedIn request and we can talk there."
- Day 7: easy out — "I'll leave it here; say the word if you ever want your market mapped."

## Voice rules (so it never sounds AI-written)
- Sound like one founder emailing another. Contractions, plain words, one idea per line.
- **Banned:** "fellow founder," "hope this finds you well," fake flattery, neat tricolon rhythm, results/% claims, calendar links in email 1.
- The ask is always tiny: **reply → I send the list → if good, connect on LinkedIn.**
