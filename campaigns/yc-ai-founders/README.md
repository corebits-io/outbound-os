# yc-ai-founders — the demo campaign (public-safe)

The campaign used in the YouTube demo. Real, recent **YC-backed AI companies** + their founders (all public data).

**Offer (founder-to-founder):** "I'll hand you your whole market as one clean list — companies that fit your ICP + the decision-makers, enriched, plus the people already posting about your problem on Reddit/LinkedIn. Want 20–30? No strings." The hook is **selectivity** (we only target products with a real, engaged audience), not results.

**This campaign ships at the `person_picked` stage on purpose:** accounts qualified, founders picked with reasons, the offer + sequence written — but **emails are blank**. We don't publish real people's emails. On camera, the live run fills them with `/find-verify-emails`, then `/preview-qa` → `/push-to-sequencer` (loads a DRAFT — never sends).

Read in order: `01`–`04` brain + `winning-emails.md` → `accounts/accounts.csv` → `prospects/prospects.csv` → `research/` → `sequences/`.

> Note: a couple of accounts are intentionally flagged to show judgment — **FuseAI** is excluded (`do_not_contact`) as a competitor (it's an outbound tool too); **Operative** is flagged for a brand pivot to "Refresh" — verify before contact.
