# 04 — Fit Examples — YC AI Founders (DEMO)

## Good-fit accounts (and WHY) — filled during sourcing
| Company | Domain | Why it's a great fit |
|---|---|---|
| Confident AI | confident-ai.com | Built open-source DeepEval (~10k stars) — a large, engaged AI-eng audience the founder already owns. |
| Asteroid | asteroid.ai | Browser-agent platform with an active AI-agent builder audience (HN, r/AI_Agents). |
| Docket | docketqa.com | QA-AI with a vocal dev/QA audience constantly posting about flaky tests. |

## Bad-fit accounts (and WHY)
| Company / type | Why it's NOT a fit |
|---|---|
| Pre-launch / stealth | Nothing to target yet; no audience to find. |
| AI product with no findable audience | The whole offer depends on a reachable audience. |
| Non-US company | Outside geography. |
| Data / list-building / lead-gen vendor | Direct competitor. |

## The pattern
- **Common traits:** YC-backed / funded, B2B AI product, recent batch, founder-led, AND a real engaged audience posting about the problem on LinkedIn/Reddit.
- **Disqualifiers:** stealth / pre-launch, no findable audience, non-US, competitors.
- **Search signals:** "YC [batch] AI", Launch YC posts, founders/products active on Reddit (r/SaaS, r/artificial, niche subs) + LinkedIn.
