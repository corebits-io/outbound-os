# 01 — Offer & ICP — YC AI Founders (DEMO campaign)

> The on-camera demo. Real offer (founder-to-founder GTM list), targeting recent YC AI companies. Public-safe: sample/redacted contacts only.

## The offer
- **What we sell (one line):** I hand founders their entire market in one clean list — every company that fits their ICP + the decision-makers, enriched — plus the people already posting about their problem on Reddit/LinkedIn.
- **What it does for them / the outcome:** their whole go-to-market in one place — usable for sales, partnerships, fundraising, or hiring — without bouncing between tools.
- **Price point / how it's sold:** founder-to-founder; open with 20–30 fit accounts free ("no strings"), then a paid build. [Confirm exact pricing.]
- **Proof / credibility:** [your proof — who you've built this for]. **Lead with selectivity, not results claims.**

## Who it's for (ICP)
- **Industries / niches:** YC-backed / recently-funded **AI companies** (B2B AI products).
- **Company size / stage:** seed–Series A, founder-led GTM (recent YC batches).
- **Geography:** US (SF-heavy), English-language.
- **The selective filter (important):** only products with a **real, engaged audience worth reaching** — people already posting about the problem the product solves on LinkedIn/Reddit. No findable audience → not a fit.
- **Trigger:** just launched / in a recent batch / raising / hiring GTM.

## Hard exclusions (never contact)
- Pre-launch stealth with nothing to target; products with no findable audience; non-US; direct competitors (data / list-building / lead-gen vendors); existing clients.

## Notes
- The hook is **selectivity** ("I only reach out to products with an audience worth going after"), not results. Copy stays very short.
