# 02 — Personas — YC AI Founders (DEMO)

## Persona A (primary)
- **Titles:** Founder / Co-founder / CEO.
- **What they own:** everything early — including GTM and the list.
- **The pain:** knows they need pipeline / market coverage; no clean list, no time to build it.
- **Why they'd reply:** founder-to-founder, specific, and we hand them something useful for free.

## Persona B (secondary)
- **Titles:** Head of Growth / first GTM hire — only if the founder is fully heads-down on product.

## Picking rules
- At YC-stage startups the **founder is the buyer** → target the founder. Use a GTM hire only if they're clearly the one running outbound.
