# Cross-account patterns — YC AI Founders

## Top patterns
1. **Founder owns GTM.** Every account is founder-led at seed — the founder IS the buyer. Lead founder-to-founder.
2. **Audience is the qualifier.** The strongest fits (Confident AI, Asteroid, Docket) have a vocal, findable audience (GitHub, HN, niche subreddits). That's the selectivity hook *and* the `{{problem}}` line.
3. **Dev / AI-infra cluster.** Most fits are devtools / AI-infra (eval, agents, QA, fine-tuning) — same audience channels, so one angle + the same proof carry across the segment.

- **A — dev / AI-infra (primary):** Confident AI, Asteroid, Docket, Kernel, Jazzberry, TrainLoop, Lemma. One founder-to-founder sequence, `{{problem}}` swapped per account.
