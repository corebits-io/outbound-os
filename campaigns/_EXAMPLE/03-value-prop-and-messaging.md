# 03 — Value Prop & Messaging

> ⚠️ FICTIONAL SAMPLE.

## Core value proposition
- **The one-sentence promise:** Get new users to first value faster, so fewer churn in the first 90 days — without adding CSM headcount.
- **The "before" state (their problem):** Signups stall in setup, CSMs onboard everyone by hand, and early churn quietly eats growth.
- **The "after" state (life with our offer):** Guided in-app onboarding does the repetitive work, activation is visible per account, and the CS team focuses on the accounts that actually need a human.

## Proof / credibility
- **Results, case studies, named clients, numbers:** ~22% median drop in 90-day churn and ~35% lift in activation across 40+ B2B SaaS customers within two quarters. Reference customers available on request.
- **Risk reversal (guarantee, pilot, free first deliverable):** 30-day pilot on one onboarding flow; we build the first flow for you. No annual commitment until the pilot clears.

## Messaging angles that have worked
> Past openers that got replies, and why.
- **Angle 1 — the hiring signal:** "Saw you're hiring 3 CSMs." Scaling CS by headcount is expensive; reframes Cohort as the cheaper way to absorb growth. Works because it's specific and timely.
- **Angle 2 — the funding signal:** "Noticed you just raised your Series B." User growth is about to spike and onboarding will strain first. Lands because it ties to something they're proud of and a problem they haven't hit yet.
- **Angle 3 — the activation benchmark:** "Most PLG teams we see activate <30% of signups." A concrete, slightly uncomfortable benchmark that makes them want to know their own number.

## Tone & rules
- **Tone:** Direct, peer-to-peer, like one operator to another. No hype.
- **Length preference:** Short — 2–4 sentences per email.
- **Words/claims we must NOT use:** No "I hope this email finds you well," no fake flattery ("love what you're building"), no specific outcome numbers promised to *them* (only cite our averages, never "you'll get 22%").
- **Call to action we prefer:** Soft interest check ("worth a quick look?" / "want me to send a 2-min teardown?") — not "book a 30-min call" in email 1.

## Competitors / alternatives they might compare us to
- Pendo, Appcues, Userpilot, Chameleon, WalkMe — and "we'll just build it in-house / keep doing it manually."
