# 01 — Offer & Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

> ⚠️ FICTIONAL SAMPLE. "Cohort" and everything below are invented to show what a filled-in brain looks like.

## The offer
- **What we sell (one line):** Cohort — a customer-onboarding platform for B2B SaaS (guided in-app flows, activation checklists, and onboarding analytics).
- **What it does for them / the outcome:** Gets new signups and new accounts to "first value" faster, so fewer churn in the first 90 days — without adding CSM headcount.
- **Price point / how it's sold:** SaaS, ~$1,500–$4,000/mo on annual contracts. Sold via a 30-min demo call.
- **Proof we can deliver (results, case studies, numbers):** Across 40+ B2B SaaS customers, median 90-day churn down ~22% and activation rate up ~35% within two quarters. Named reference customers available on request.

## Who it's for (ICP)
- **Industries / niches:** B2B SaaS with a self-serve trial or product-led / hybrid motion (not pure sales-led).
- **Company size (employees or revenue):** 50–500 employees / roughly $5M–$50M ARR. Big enough to have a CS function and budget; not enterprise-procurement-heavy.
- **Geography:** US, UK, Canada, Western Europe (English-language outreach).
- **Tech / tools they use that signal a fit:** Intercom, Pendo, Appcues, Userpilot, Segment, Amplitude — or notably *no* onboarding tooling (doing it manually in-app + email).
- **Business situation or trigger that makes now a good time:** Just raised (Series A/B), hiring CSMs / Customer Onboarding Managers, launching or expanding a self-serve motion, public complaints/reviews about slow setup or activation.

## Hard exclusions (never contact)
- **Industries/sizes to avoid:** <50 employees (no CS team/budget), non-SaaS, agencies/services, on-prem or pure sales-led software with no trial.
- **Competitors / existing clients:** Pendo, Appcues, Userpilot, Chameleon, WalkMe (direct competitors). Current Cohort customers.
- **Anything else:** Companies with no self-serve/trial motion at all — there's no onboarding funnel for us to improve.

## Notes
- Strongest fit = a PLG/hybrid SaaS that is *scaling user volume faster than its CS team can manually onboard*. The hiring + funding signals usually mean exactly that.
- "Doing onboarding manually" is a buying signal, not a disqualifier — it's the pain we remove.
