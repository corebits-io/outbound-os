# Winning emails — Cohort (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

> The 2–3 short emails `write-copy` learns Cohort's voice + angles from. Real, short, reply-getting.

## Email 1 — hiring-signal angle
- **Subject:** the 3 CSM hires
- **Body:** Hi {{first_name}} — saw you're hiring 3 CSMs. Scaling onboarding by headcount works until the next growth spurt, then you're hiring again. We get new accounts to first value on their own — most teams cut 90-day churn ~20% without adding CSMs. Worth a quick look?
- **Why it worked:** specific, true hook (the hires); reframes the cost; soft ask, no calendar link.

## Email 2 — activation-benchmark angle
- **Subject:** most PLG teams activate <30%
- **Body:** {{first_name}} — most PLG teams we see activate under 30% of signups, and never find out why. Want a 2-minute teardown of where {{company}}'s new users likely stall?
- **Why it worked:** a slightly uncomfortable, concrete benchmark → makes them want to know their own number.

## Notes on voice / rules
- Direct, peer-to-peer, 2–4 sentences. No "hope this finds you well," no fake flattery. Soft interest-check CTA, not "book a 30-min call."
