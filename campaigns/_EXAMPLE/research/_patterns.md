# Cross-account patterns

> ⚠️ FICTIONAL SAMPLE. Written after researching the batch.

## Top 3 patterns
1. **Scaling CS by headcount.** The strongest-fit accounts (FlowMetrics, Northwind, Northstar) are all *hiring CSMs/onboarding roles* — they're absorbing onboarding load with people. The pitch writes itself: same activation, less headcount.
2. **Post-funding user wave.** A cluster (Stackwell, plus others that recently raised) is about to spike signups via self-serve expansion. The pain is *imminent*, not current — angle is "before it hits."
3. **Vacant-seat accounts.** A few (Cartwheel) are *building* CS and have no leader yet. The owner of activation is Product/Growth today, not CS. Person-picking has to adapt.

## Proposed segments
- **Segment A — "Scaling CS" (hiring signal):** FlowMetrics, Northwind Cloud, Northstar Billing, Nimbus Desk. Lead with the hiring observation. → primary sequence.
- **Segment B — "Post-raise wave" (funding signal):** Stackwell, Ledgerly. Lead with the funding observation.
- **Segment C — "Product owns activation" (PLG/vacant-CS):** Cartwheel, Meridian Labs, Pixelware, Tempo, Brightloop. Target Product/Growth; lead with the self-serve/freemium activation gap.

## Shared language they use
"Activation," "time to value," "self-serve," "onboarding load." Mirror these words in the copy.

> For this sample, the built sequence covers **Segment A** (`sequences/sequence-scaling-cs.md`). B and C would each get their own angle on the same skeleton.
