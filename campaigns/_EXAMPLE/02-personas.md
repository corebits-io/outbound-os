# 02 — Personas (who to contact inside the account)

> ⚠️ FICTIONAL SAMPLE.

## Persona A (primary)
- **Titles / variations:** Head of Customer Success, VP Customer Success, Director of Customer Success, Head of Customer Experience.
- **What they own / are responsible for:** Retention, net revenue retention, activation, the CS team's capacity.
- **The pain they feel that our offer solves:** New accounts stall in setup; CSMs are buried in manual onboarding; 90-day churn is too high and they can't hire their way out of it.
- **What they care about (metrics, pressures):** Churn %, NRR, activation rate, CSM-to-account ratio, time-to-value.
- **Why they'd reply:** We directly move the number they're measured on (churn/activation) without more headcount.

## Persona B (secondary)
- **Titles / variations:** Head of Product, VP Product, Director of Product-Led Growth, Head of Growth.
- **What they own:** Activation and the in-product onboarding experience; trial-to-paid conversion.
- **The pain:** Signups don't reach the aha moment; activation is flat; engineering time for onboarding UX is scarce.
- **Why they'd reply:** Cohort ships onboarding flows without burning their eng roadmap.

## Persona C (optional / champion or gatekeeper)
- **Titles / variations:** Customer Onboarding Manager, Onboarding Lead, Implementation Manager.
- **Role in the decision:** Feels the manual pain daily and champions internally — great for a "who owns this?" referral, usually reports to Persona A.

## Picking rules
- For a small company (<100 staff): activation often sits with **Head of Product** or a founder — lead there.
- For a mid-size company (100–500): the buyer is usually **Head/VP of Customer Success** — lead there.
- Tie-breakers / signals that change who we pick: if they're actively **hiring a Customer Onboarding Manager**, target whoever that role reports to (usually Persona A). If the trigger is a self-serve launch, lean Persona B.
