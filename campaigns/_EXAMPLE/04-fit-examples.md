# 04 — Fit Examples (teach the niche)

> ⚠️ FICTIONAL SAMPLE. Companies below are invented to illustrate the pattern.

## Good-fit accounts (and WHY each is a fit)
| Company | Domain | Why it's a great fit |
|---|---|---|
| FlowMetrics | flowmetrics.io | PLG analytics SaaS, ~120 staff, hiring 3 CSMs — scaling CS manually right now. |
| Stackwell | stackwell.com | DevOps SaaS, ~200 staff, just raised a $40M Series B — user growth about to spike. |
| Cartwheel | cartwheel.app | E-commerce SaaS, ~90 staff, hiring a VP of Customer Success — building the CS function. |
| Northwind Cloud | northwindcloud.com | Infra SaaS, ~250 staff, open roles for both Onboarding Manager and CSM — clear activation pain. |
| Nimbus Desk | nimbusdesk.com | Helpdesk SaaS, ~110 staff, G2 reviews mention "slow to get set up" — public activation pain. |

## Bad-fit accounts (and WHY each is wrong)
> Just as important. The "why" teaches the boundaries.
| Company | Domain | Why it's NOT a fit |
|---|---|---|
| Anvil Forge | anvilforge.com | On-prem manufacturing software, no trial/self-serve motion — no onboarding funnel for us to improve. |
| Quill | quill.so | Only ~45 staff — below the size where there's a CS team or budget. |
| Pendo | pendo.io | Direct competitor (does onboarding tooling). |
| Brightside Agency | brightside.agency | A services agency, not a SaaS product. |

## The pattern (operator fills this in after reviewing the above)
- **Common traits across good fits:** B2B SaaS, 50–500 staff, self-serve/PLG or hybrid motion, AND a live trigger — hiring CS/onboarding roles or fresh funding.
- **Disqualifiers seen across bad fits:** <50 staff, no trial/self-serve motion, on-prem, agencies/services, direct competitors.
- **Signals/keywords to search for when sourcing:** "hiring Customer Success Manager", "hiring Customer Onboarding Manager", "Series A/B [SaaS]", "free trial" + "[category] software", review-site complaints about setup/activation time.
