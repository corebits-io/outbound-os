# Glossary — plain English

- **ICP** — Ideal Customer Profile: the kind of company you should target.
- **Persona** — the kind of *person* inside that company you contact (role, what they own).
- **Signal / intent** — a trigger that makes now a good time to reach out (funding, hiring, a launch, posting about the problem).
- **Sourcing** — finding companies that match the ICP.
- **Enrichment** — finding contact data (email, phone) for a person.
- **Waterfall** — trying enrichment tools in order, stopping at the first valid hit (so you don't pay twice).
- **Verification / validation** — checking an email is real before sending, to avoid bounces.
- **Deliverable / catch-all / undeliverable** — verification results. Deliverable = safe; catch-all = risky-ish; undeliverable = don't send.
- **Confidence tag** — `verified` (validated), `likely` (found, not fully validated), `guess` (pattern only — never send).
- **Sequencer** — the tool that sends the emails on a schedule (Instantly, Smartlead).
- **Sequence / cadence** — the series of emails + the days between them.
- **Warmup** — gradually building a new inbox's reputation so it lands in inbox, not spam.
- **SPF / DKIM / DMARC** — DNS records that prove your mail is legitimate.
- **Bounce** — an email that couldn't be delivered. High bounce = deliverability damage.
- **Merge field / custom variable** — `{{first_name}}`-style placeholders filled per lead.
- **Draft vs. activate** — loading a campaign (draft) is not sending; activating starts the sending.
