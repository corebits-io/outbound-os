# Swipe file — subject lines that get opened

> Patterns, not templates. The best subject is short, specific, and looks like a human typed it to one person.

## What works
- **Short + lowercase** — 2–5 words, like an internal note. ("quick question", "your market, one list")
- **Specific to them** — reference the real hook. ("the 3 CSM hires", "investors looking at battery storage")
- **Curiosity, not hype** — a small open loop, no clickbait.
- **A plain question** — ("worth a look?", "you handling this already?")
- **Empty subject on follow-ups** — replies thread under email 1.

## Avoid
- ALL CAPS, exclamation marks, "FREE", "guarantee", "$$$" — spam triggers.
- Anything that screams mass-send ("Dear Sir/Madam", "{{Company}}!!!").
- Long subjects that get truncated on mobile.

## Examples (adapt — don't copy verbatim)
- `fellow founder — built you a list`
- `the {{signal}}` (e.g. "the Series B")
- `{{company}} + the right investors`
- `quick one on {{problem}}`
- `should I close this out?` (breakup)
