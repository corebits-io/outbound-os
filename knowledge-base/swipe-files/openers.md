# Swipe file — openers that earn a reply

> The first 1–2 lines decide everything. Lead with *them*, not you. Probe, don't pitch.

## Patterns that work
- **The research hook** — one true, specific observation: "Saw {{company}} {{signal}}." Proves you're not mass-sending.
- **Founder-to-founder / peer** — "I'm a founder too, and I only reach out when…" (selectivity as the hook).
- **The signal → relevance** — tie their trigger to why you're reaching out *now*.
- **The uncomfortable benchmark** — a concrete stat that makes them want their own number ("most PLG teams activate <30%…").
- **Short + one ask** — under ~100 words, one easy yes ("worth a quick look?", "want me to send 20–30?").

## Avoid
- "Hope this email finds you well." / "I hope you're doing well."
- Fake flattery ("love what you're building!") with nothing specific.
- A wall of text or three asks. A calendar link in email 1.
- Claiming results you can't prove.

## Mini-examples
- "Hey {{first_name}} — saw {{company}} {{signal}}. Usually that means {{implication}}…"
- "I'm a founder too, and I only reach out when a product clearly has an audience worth going after. {{company}} does."
- "No pitch — just think there's a fit. We focus only on {{niche}}, so {{benefit}}."
