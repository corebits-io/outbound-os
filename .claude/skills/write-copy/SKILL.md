# Write copy

Write email #1 that earns a reply — specific, short, human.

**Read:** the prospect's `research/<domain>.md`, `03-value-prop-and-messaging.md`, and `winning-emails.md`.
**Write:** the draft opener(s); list any personalization fields in `sequences/custom-fields.md`.

## Rules
- **Short + punchy** — aim under ~100 words, one clear ask. **Probe, don't pitch.**
- Lead with one true, specific observation (the research hook), not `{{first_name}}` alone.
- Match the voice + angles in `winning-emails.md`. **No fabricated results or "it worked" claims.**
- Self-check: "could this be sent to 1,000 companies unchanged?" If yes, it's too generic — add the hook.
